A quiet place with beautiful vistas

Located on the waterfront at Little Norfolk Bay, Four Seasons Waterfront Villas is ideally positioned for exploring Port Arthur and the Tasman Peninsula. Enjoy a peaceful waterfront setting while staying within easy reach of the region’s historic sites, coastal walks and natural attractions.

Set across a peaceful waterfront property, each self-contained villa offers its own kitchen, living and dining areas, private entrance and easy access to the Little Norfolk Bay waterfront.

The Tasman Peninsula is home to an abundance of native wildlife, including wallabies, echidnas and a wide variety of birdlife. With plenty to see and do nearby, Four Seasons Waterfront Villas is a comfortable base for couples and families exploring the region.

Comfortable Villas

Each villa is fully self-contained, with its own kitchen, dining and living spaces, and easy access to the Little Norfolk Bay waterfront.

Coastal Vista

Set directly on Little Norfolk Bay, the property offers peaceful water views and easy access to the foreshore.

Explore the Tasman Peninsula

Port Arthur Historic Site, spectacular coastal walks and some of Tasmania’s best-known natural attractions are all within easy reach.
