Nearby Attractions

Explore the wonders of the Tasman Peninsula

Four Seasons Waterfront Villas is ideally positioned for exploring some of the Tasman Peninsula’s best-known historic sites, coastal walks and natural attractions.

Historic Sites

Port Arthur Historic Site

Part of the UNESCO World Heritage-listed Australian Convict Sites, Port Arthur Historic Site is one of Tasmania’s most significant historic attractions and is just a short drive from the villas.

Eaglehawk Neck

A narrow isthmus connecting the Forestier and Tasman peninsulas, Eaglehawk Neck is known for its convict-era Dog Line and nearby coastal landmarks.

Coal Mines Historic Site

Explore the ruins of Tasmania’s first operational coal mine, where convicts once worked underground at Plunkett Point. The World Heritage-listed site includes remains of the penitentiary, underground cells and mine workings in a striking coastal setting.

Natural Wonders

Pirates Bay Lookout

Panoramic views across Pirates Bay and the rugged coastline make this an easy scenic stop while exploring Eaglehawk Neck.

Tessellated Pavement

A remarkable natural rock formation on the foreshore at Eaglehawk Neck, where the fractured sandstone creates a distinctive tiled appearance.

Three Capes Track

A spectacular 48 km, four-day coastal walk through Tasman National Park, taking in Cape Pillar and Cape Hauy, with dramatic dolerite cliffs, forests and coastal heath.

Remarkable Cave

A spectacular sea cave near Port Arthur, shaped by the Southern Ocean and accessed via a short walk and staircase to the viewing platform.

Cape Raoul

A challenging 14 km return walk through forest and coastal heath to the dramatic cliffs of Cape Raoul, with expansive views across the Southern Ocean.

Tasmans Arch

A striking natural rock arch carved by the sea near Eaglehawk Neck, showcasing the dramatic coastal geology of the Tasman Peninsula.
